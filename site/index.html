How it plays

Built for a crowded couch

Late hits 03

Three seconds after the whistle

The ref spots the ball. You have three seconds to get back up and return the favor. Both teams can move and hit. The clock, score and ball spot are already settled.

  • BShove (hold Turbo for a shoulder hit)
  • XDive at a standing opponent or drop an elbow on a downed one
  • AVote for the next play. If everyone presses it, play calling starts early.

Fury

Fill the meter. Catch fire.

Big defensive stops and explosive gains fill a team's Fury meter. When it's full, the whole team catches fire for three plays: faster, endless turbo, and every tackle is a big hit.

1–8 players

Eight seats, one screen

Up to four people per team, in any mix of gamepads and the two halves of one keyboard. Everyone presses A to take a seat, steps left or right to pick a side, and presses A again when ready. The computer runs any spot nobody takes. Teammates rotate play-calling duties each drive.

Playing alone? Quick Solo puts you in one-minute quarters against a Rookie opponent. Computer skill goes up to Pro and All-Fury.

7

Seven on seven

A 100-yard field, seven a side, no penalties. Four quarters of 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00, and the clock only runs while the ball is live.

30

Thirty yards for a first down

Four downs to make 30. Touchdown is 6, then kick the extra point or go for two from the 3. Field goal 3, safety 2. Ties go to sudden death.

A diagrammed playbook

Pass, run & trick, and kick pages. Flip any play. Against a computer defense, hold LB before the snap to see your routes on the turf.

Play Film

Replay all fourteen jerseys and the football from the last snap, right through the final late hit. Scrub the timeline. At the final score, replay the longest touchdown.

Grab a friend. Split the keyboard.

One of you on W A S D, the other on the arrow keys. Plug in more pads for up to eight.

In the browser: the web version runs in desktop browsers with a keyboard or gamepads. Browsers only report a gamepad after you press one of its buttons, so press any button on each pad once the game has loaded.

How it was made

Built from scratch

4th & Fury is an original game in the spirit of the 90s arcade football cabinets: its own name, teams, art, sound and playbook.

  • Unreal Engine 5 runs the original game, in C++ with no Blueprints. The rules live in a plain C++ simulation.
  • Blender builds the athletes by script, in three builds, with 52 animations, plus the stadium and crowd.
  • Python draws the team emblems, field and interface art, and synthesizes every sound effect, the crowd and the music.
  • Three.js powers the browser version, a port of the game for the web.
