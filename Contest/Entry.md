**One-line pitch**

Seven-a-side arcade football for one to eight people on a single screen, with four downs to gain thirty yards and three seconds to settle things after the whistle.

**Entry description**

The first down marker is thirty yards away. Your friend calls a deep crossing route, you hit the receiver in stride, and the last defender drives you into the turf one yard short. The ref spots the ball. You have three seconds to get back up and return the favor.

4th & Fury puts two benches on the same screen. Choose from eight original teams, diagrammed plays and a mix of human and computer teammates. Teammates rotate play-calling duties each drive. Big defensive stops and explosive gains build Fury, giving a team three plays of unlimited turbo. The play film can replay all fourteen jerseys and the football from the last snap, right through the final late hit. The final scoreboard lets you replay the longest touchdown.

**Production disclosure**

The rosters, emblems, uniforms, stadium, animation and playbook are original to this project. The Blender and Python source in the repository generates the meshes, rigs, textures, music and effects. The announcer and field calls include synthesized performances generated with Qwen3-TTS using the Ryan preset. AI-assisted development was used. Font licenses accompany the project and the Mac build. The entrant should review these credits when completing the festival’s rights and production disclosures.
