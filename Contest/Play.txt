4TH & FURY
Mac play guide • Version 0.9.0

Open FourthAndFury.app on an Apple Silicon Mac. The game runs offline.

YOUR FIRST DRIVE

Press X on a controller, F on the left keyboard or J on the right keyboard
for Quick Solo. You get one-minute quarters against a Rookie opponent.
The team on the left of the score display attacks to the right when it
has the ball. The team on the right attacks to the left.

Pick a diagram in the playbook and confirm with A. Against the computer,
hold the left shoulder before the snap to see your routes on the turf.
Press A to snap. At quarterback, press the button floating above the
receiver you want. The catch-point ring shows where the ball will land.
On a run, hold a trigger to sprint. Gain thirty yards in four downs to
earn a fresh set.

BRING ANOTHER PLAYER

Choose Set Up Match from the title screen. Each player presses A on their
own input, picks a side and presses A again to ready up. Up to four seats
can join each side. Pick the teams and lock them in. Face-button prompts match each controller.
The computer fills the rest of each lineup. Teammates take turns calling the next drive.

AFTER THE WHISTLE

You have three seconds. B shoves, Turbo + B lands a shoulder hit and X
dives or drops an elbow. Every player can press A to advance early.
Pause after a play and press Y to watch Play Film. The timeline marks the
whistle. Move left or right to scrub through the late hits. At the final
score, Y replays the longest touchdown of the match.

Press Start to pause. X opens the Field Guide. Settings has graphics and
six sound channels. Turn on Announcer Captions in Sound to follow the
commentary with the volume down. Balanced adjusts field resolution toward
120 FPS or your lower frame cap. Reduced Motion steadies the camera and
turns off impact shake and stadium flashes. Quit Game closes the app.
